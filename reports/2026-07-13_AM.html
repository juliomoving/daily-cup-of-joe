IF THIS RHYMES WITH 2000
Nasdaq fell -78.0% peak→trough (2000-03-10 → 2002-10-09, ~31 months); it took ~15 years to reclaim the high.

The froth is concentrated, not broad. Semiconductors are priced for perfection (SOX P/E ~59x, essentially at its comparison peak) and the market's protection/positioning signals — a 16-handle VIX, loose NFCI, historically tight IG and Baa credit — scream complacency, exactly the conditions that precede late-cycle reversals. Valuation breadth, however, is nothing like 2000: the Nasdaq's ~40x trailing multiple and modest 10.5% premium to its 200DMA are a fraction of dot-com extremes. Concentration (~38% top-10, analyst estimate) and a ~205% market-cap/GDP 'Buffett' ratio (analyst estimate) say the index is expensive and top-heavy, but the absence of a parabolic multiple and the still-firm PMIs (ISM Mfg 53.3, Svcs 54.0) argue the cycle can extend. The risk is that an exogenous shock — the Iran oil spike feeding an inflation re-acceleration — is precisely the kind of late-cycle catalyst these signposts are built to warn about.
