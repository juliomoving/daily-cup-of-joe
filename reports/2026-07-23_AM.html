🧮Fixed Income Spreadscomputed

Credit is the dog that isn't barking. US IG OAS is 77bps (0.77%), a z-score of -0.84 and just the 2nd percentile of history; US HY is 267bps (2.67%), z -0.9 and the 4th percentile — both near the tightest levels ever, versus a HY range of 230-1970bps. Translation: bond investors are pricing near-zero recession or default risk and demanding almost no extra compensation for corporate risk. That complacency is the vulnerability. For spreads to signal real stress, IG would need to widen back toward ~120bps (its own historical average area) and HY through ~400-450bps — moves that typically require the oil shock to morph into a growth or funding shock. Until credit cracks, equity dips are corrections, not crises; watch HY OAS as the tell.
