Nasdaq fell -78.0% peak→trough (2000-03-10 → 2002-10-09, ~31 months); it took ~15 years to reclaim the high.

The useful history is the drawdown, not the peak. From 2000-03-10 to 2002-10-09 the Nasdaq fell 78.0%, from 5,048.62 to 1,114.11, and it took 31 months. The current cycle, anchored at the October 2022 low, has taken the index from 100 to 239.6 over 48 months. It is whether the trigger comes from valuation, as in 2000, or from credit and leverage, as in 2007 - and every computed indicator that is already past its 2000 condition points at the second.
