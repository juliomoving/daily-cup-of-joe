The tell of this market is the split between price and psychology. Semiconductors carry mania-grade multiples and complacency gauges (low VIX, tight spreads, easy financial conditions) sit near their frothiest — yet the broad index P/E, the price stretch, retail sentiment (AAII net −0.9pp), and consumer confidence (91.2, well off its 140.8 peak) are not euphoric. That is a concentrated, AI-driven advance riding on cheap credit, not a wall-to-wall bubble. It can run — narrow leadership and loose conditions often persist — but the fragility is that so much of the froth is banked in one group (chips) and in the assumption that spreads stay at record tights through a live war. Watch the semis multiple, IG spreads, and VIX for the first crack.
