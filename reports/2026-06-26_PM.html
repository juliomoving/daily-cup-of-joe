📋PM Edition — What You Need to Know

“Risk wobbles as Tokyo cracks and Warsh hangs hawkish: Nikkei sheds 4.2%, oil bleeds on Iran de-escalation, gold and defensives catch the bid”

A quiet US tape (S&P 500 -0.05% to 7,354.02, Dow -0.09%, Nasdaq Comp -0.24%) masked a far more violent session abroad: the Nikkei 225 collapsed 4.15% (-3,005.46 to 69,360.88), its worst day in this cycle, even with the yen pinned near 161.74 — a Japan-specific risk-off that bears watching given a JGB 10Y sticky at 2.621%. Stateside the story was rotation, not retreat — Health Care ripped +3.16% on the day (+7.9% on the week) and rate-sensitives (Real Estate +1.43%, Utilities +0.84%) and staples led, while Info Tech (-1.05%) and Industrials (-1.53%) lagged; the equal-weight tell was Russell 2000 eking out +0.07% as megacap growth wobbled. The macro backdrop softened: the US-Iran MOU (signed Jun 17, 60-day nuclear timeline) and the lifted Hormuz blockade crushed crude — WTI -3.74% to 69.23, Brent -2.92% to 73.06 — even as sporadic US strikes on Iranian drone sites kept a tail risk alive. Bonds bull-flattened modestly (2Y -3.1bp to 4.0921, 10Y -2.4bp to 4.3686; 2s10s +27.65bps computed), gold jumped +1.54% to 4,088.74 and silver +2.23% to 59.15 on a softer dollar (DXY -0.06% to 101.366). Overlaying it all: new Fed Chair Warsh held rates at 3.50-3.75% at his June debut but the FOMC dot median tilted to a HIKE and he scrapped forward guidance — a hawkish-uncertainty regime that keeps the inflation watch live. Net tone: complacent in the US (VIX -2.54% to 18.41), fragile at the margins.

Bubble-analog — today's Nasdaq vs the dot-com cycle (two anchors)

WHY TWO START DATES?
Each line is rebased to 100 at a low the tech bull launched from, so elapsed time lines up. Oct-2022 is today's anchor — it was the prior bear-market bottom. Oct-1990 is the dot-com cycle's equivalent (its literal prior bear bottom). But 1990 predates the internet mania by ~4 years, so the second chart re-anchors the dot-com line to the 1994 reset low, just before the surge ignited, for a tighter like-for-like. Today sits very early under the strict 1990 analog, further along under the 1994 surge analog.

IF THIS RHYMES WITH 2000
Nasdaq fell -78.0% peak→trough (2000-03-10 → 2002-10-09, ~31 months); it took ~15 years to reclaim the high.

The dot-com analog is sobering but not mechanical. The 2000 episode drew down 78% over 31 months from a 5,048.62 peak; today's Nasdaq sits 99th-percentile rich (z 2.92) and is already slipping below its 50DMA with a 60% empirical death-cross reversal probability. The froth is real but narrow — semis (SOX 73x), top-10 concentration ~37%, CAPE ~39.7, Buffett ~235.6% — while the breadth, IPO mania (froth ~45/100, an analyst estimate; the current pipeline is active but orderly, not 1999-style), and consumer euphoria that defined the true peak are absent. The risk is therefore not an imminent 78% crater but a leadership unwind: today's rotation OUT of Info Tech/Comm Services and INTO health care and defensives is exactly how narrow late-cycle markets begin to crack. Elevated long-term EPS-growth expectations (~70/100, an analyst estimate and a BofA bear-market signpost) are the fuel that makes that unwind violent if AI earnings disappoint.
