WHY TWO START DATES?
Each line is rebased to 100 at a low the tech bull launched from, so elapsed time lines up. Oct-2022 is today's anchor — it was the prior bear-market bottom. Oct-1990 is the dot-com cycle's equivalent (its literal prior bear bottom). But 1990 predates the internet mania by ~4 years, so the second chart re-anchors the dot-com line to the 1994 reset low, just before the surge ignited, for a tighter like-for-like. Today sits very early under the strict 1990 analog, further along under the 1994 surge analog.

IF THIS RHYMES WITH 2000
Nasdaq fell -78.0% peak→trough (2000-03-10 → 2002-10-09, ~31 months); it took ~15 years to reclaim the high.

The drawdown reference frames the stakes: the 2000-2002 Nasdaq bear ran -78% over 31 months from the Mar-2000 peak. The dual-anchor analogs (the 1990 bear-bottom and 1994 reset) both show the dot-com path going parabolic only in its final ~12-18 months before the peak; the current path (anchored Oct-2022, index 242.8 at month 44) is elevated but has not gone vertical the way the 1990-anchored analog did into month 113. The actionable read: this is a market where the valuation extremes are concentrated (semis, mega-cap, credit) and the complacency is in volatility/financial conditions, while the classic broad-euphoria tells (retail sentiment, leverage surge, IPO indiscipline) are not yet ringing — a setup that can run further but is increasingly fragile to any catalyst that cracks the AI-leadership or credit-complacency story.
