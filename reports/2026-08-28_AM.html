The useful way to hold this is that today's market is less mad about price and more reckless about risk than 2000 was. So the sequencing I'd expect is: retail capitulates into the theme, ISM rolls, credit finally reprices, and only then does the multiple matter. None of the first three has happened. This is an expensive market with no safety margin, not yet a bursting one — but the margin, not the price, is what I'd be monitoring.
