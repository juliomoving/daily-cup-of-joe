Five charts tracking the mechanism Simon White laid out for why Treasury liquidity is deteriorating: rising inflation volatility feeds into a higher term premium, which steepens the curve, which raises bond vol, which shows up as worse UST market liquidity. Latest reading is +0.84 vol points (69th percentile of the 13 runs recorded so far). Positive means the 5%-OTM put is pricier than the at-the-money option -- the market paying up for downside-price / higher-yield insurance on long-end Treasuries.

Fed staff's own DKW decomposition (D'Amico-Kim-Wei, updated Kim-Walsh-Wei) splits the rise in long yields into a policy-uncertainty component (real term premium) and an inflation-uncertainty component -- both rising is the article's read that this move isn't just a rate-expectations story.
