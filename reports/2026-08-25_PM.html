The whole session hangs off one wire: crude cracked. Brent fell 5.49% to $87.11 and WTI 3.12% to $82.36 as the market priced progress in the US–Iran talks — Trump has canceled planned strikes citing progress, contingent on 'being able to rapidly make a DEAL,' even as a senior IRGC adviser said Tehran could 'prolong' the war (CNN, Aug 12; Al Jazeera, Aug 22-24). The computed distributions are the story. US HY is at z -0.90 and the 5th percentile, range 233bp to 1971bp: 35bp of tightening available, 1703bp of widening. EUR IG is z -0.88 / 7th percentile (range 46-469bp), EUR HY z -0.72 / 9th percentile (range 184-2144bp), and EM USD is the most extreme of the lot at z -1.36 and the 3rd percentile, only 23bp above its 139bp all-time tight against a 1087bp high. Every crisis-comparison percentile — GFC 2008, EuroDebt 2011, COVID 2020, Hike 2022 — computes at 0, i.e. spreads are not fractionally along the path to any prior stress episode. Key levels and what it takes to reach them: US HY at 300bp requires only a modest growth scare or a failed Hormuz negotiation — that is roughly 32bp, a two-week move in any ordinary risk-off; 350bp needs an actual default-cycle catalyst, most plausibly HY energy issuers if crude round-trips lower or shipping costs stay punitive; 450bp+ is a recession print, and the negative -23k payroll number is the first data point in months that makes that non-trivial. On IG, 100bp is the level that would signal the investment-grade market has stopped treating a 5.17% 30-year as benign; it takes either heavy supply into a deficit running at 5.99% of GDP or a genuine hawkish repricing from Warsh. The asymmetry is the entire point: at the 5th percentile you are being paid almost nothing to hold the risk that spreads normalize, let alone that they widen.

Five charts tracking the mechanism Simon White laid out for why Treasury liquidity is deteriorating: rising inflation volatility feeds into a higher term premium, which steepens the curve, which raises bond vol, which shows up as worse UST market liquidity. Latest reading is +0.74 vol points (20th percentile of the 5 runs recorded so far). Positive means the 5%-OTM put is pricier than the at-the-money option -- the market paying up for downside-price / higher-yield insurance on long-end Treasuries.

Fed staff's own DKW decomposition (D'Amico-Kim-Wei, updated Kim-Walsh-Wei) splits the rise in long yields into a policy-uncertainty component (real term premium) and an inflation-uncertainty component -- both rising is the article's read that this move isn't just a rate-expectations story.
