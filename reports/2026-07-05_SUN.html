Bubble-analog — today's Nasdaq vs the dot-com cycle (two anchors)

WHY TWO START DATES?
Each line is rebased to 100 at a low the tech bull launched from, so elapsed time lines up. Oct-2022 is today's anchor — it was the prior bear-market bottom. Oct-1990 is the dot-com cycle's equivalent (its literal prior bear bottom). But 1990 predates the internet mania by ~4 years, so the second chart re-anchors the dot-com line to the 1994 reset low, just before the surge ignited, for a tighter like-for-like. Today sits very early under the strict 1990 analog, further along under the 1994 surge analog.

IF THIS RHYMES WITH 2000
Nasdaq fell -78.0% peak→trough (2000-03-10 → 2002-10-09, ~31 months); it took ~15 years to reclaim the high.

The 2000 analog matters because the drawdown that followed was -78% over 31 months (peak Mar-2000, trough Oct-2002). Against that, my analyst-estimate context: S&P top-10 concentration ~39% (historically extreme and the market's single biggest structural fragility), Shiller CAPE ~39x (rich, roughly 1929/2021 territory but below 2000's ~44x), the Buffett market-cap/GDP ratio ~205% (well above every prior cycle), IPO froth ~45/100 (active but nowhere near the 2000 blow-off), and long-term S&P EPS-growth expectations ~70/100 (elevated — the BofA-style signpost is amber). The tell is that leadership is cracking from the top down (Info Tech -9.58% 1m) while breadth improves into financials and defensives — a rotation, not yet a rout. The danger is not that valuations are at 2000 levels; it is that concentration and credit complacency leave no cushion if the megacap-AI earnings narrative disappoints.

NEAR TERM
