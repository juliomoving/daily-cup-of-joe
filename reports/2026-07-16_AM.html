The Dallas Fed breadth data is the honest read and it cuts both ways: 24% of the PCE basket is inflating above 5% (14% above 10% plus 10% in the 5-10% band) and a further 34% sits in the 3-5% band — call it 58% of the basket running above target — while only 14.8% is in the 0-3% zone and 27.2% is outright falling. The offset is the wage side, which is genuinely cooling: Average Hourly Earnings at 3.5% and ECI at 0.9% YoY are consistent with 2% inflation, not 3%, and with Unemployment at 4.2%, JOLTS Openings at 7,594k, Quits at 2.0 and Nonfarm Payrolls at just 57k, the labour market is not generating a wage-price spiral. Markets agree: the 5y breakeven at 2.2761% is close to target-consistent and the 1y at 1.0855% is remarkably low — the bond market thinks the near-term inflation problem is essentially solved. My verdict sides with the breadth data over the breakevens. Expect core to grind in the 2.5-3.0% zone rather than return to 2%, which means the new Fed chair inherits a miss he has already promised to fix.
