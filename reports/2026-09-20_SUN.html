This is the most consequential and least discussed thing on today's page. At the 16 September press conference Warsh announced a 25bp increase in the target range to 3.75–4.00%. Two structural changes matter as much as the rate. First, he is reshaping the form and function of forward guidance, reflecting a long-standing discomfort with pre-announcing future decisions; markets that have spent fifteen years trading the dot plot and the statement language now have to trade the data instead.
