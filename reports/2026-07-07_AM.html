IF THIS RHYMES WITH 2000
Nasdaq fell -78.0% peak→trough (2000-03-10 → 2002-10-09, ~31 months); it took ~15 years to reclaim the high.

The 2000 analogy holds where it hurts: leadership breadth is dangerously narrow (top-10 ~38%, semis at 97% of peak froth) and investors are complacent (VIX ~16, spreads at record tights, put/call sanguine). It breaks where it helps: aggregate Nasdaq P/E is a fraction of 2000, the yield curve is positive not inverted, PMIs are still growing, and AAII sentiment is actually net-bearish (-10.9), which is not euphoria. The drawdown reference — the 2000-02 Nasdaq fell 78% over 31 months — is the tail scenario, not the base case. The realistic risk is a semis/megacap-led air-pocket (the computed 60% Nasdaq reversal frequency) that credit, priced for perfection, would then amplify. Watch the SOX, the top-10 weight, and any crack in HY spreads off their 2.30 floor as the early-warning trio.
