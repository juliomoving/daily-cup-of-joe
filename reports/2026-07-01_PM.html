🏦Fed Chair Warsh
NEAR TERM

At the next FOMC meeting, expect a hold with a hawkish bias. In his first meeting as chair (June 17, 2026) the Fed left rates unchanged and signaled higher rates ahead, with 9 of 18 committee members backing hikes this year (6 of them favoring two quarter-point increases) and only one member expecting a cut. Warsh used his first press conference to stress determination to return inflation to 2%. Base case: no cut; a live risk of a hike if inflation data (Core PCE 3.41%) stays hot or the Iran energy shock worsens.

~1 YEAR

Over ~12 months, Warsh is likely to run policy tighter-for-longer than Trump wants. His long-standing record favors tighter money and a smaller, less-interventionist Fed balance sheet; his stated 'new vision' points to hawkish credibility-building rather than the cuts the White House expected.
