IF THIS RHYMES WITH 2000
Nasdaq fell -78.0% peak→trough (2000-03-10 → 2002-10-09, ~31 months); it took ~15 years to reclaim the high.

Today's tape made the point: a single-group (semis/megacap tech) unwind dragged the indices while defensives and credit shrugged — exactly what a narrow, concentration-driven froth looks like when it wobbles. With the top-10 S&P weight near ~38% (analyst estimate), Shiller CAPE ~40.3x (analyst estimate, ~25% above its long-run average), the Buffett market-cap/GDP ratio ~234% (analyst estimate) and elevated long-term EPS-growth expectations (~72/100, analyst estimate, a BofA bear-market signpost), the valuation backdrop is genuinely 2000-rhyme. The 2002 dot-com analog (computed) is a -78% peak-to-trough over 31 months — a reminder of the downside if the semis P/E (already above 2000 conditions) and record-tight credit both re-rate. But the curve is positively sloped (+30bps), spreads are at record tights with no crisis signal, and sentiment is restrained — so the base case remains 'expensive and vulnerable to a sharp tech correction,' not 'imminent systemic bubble burst.' Micron's print tomorrow is the near-term referendum on the AI/semis leg.

De-escalation is the dominant force on crude. Net for markets: the war premium is draining (Brent -1.3% today to 76.87), but Hormuz headline risk remains a live two-way catalyst for oil.
