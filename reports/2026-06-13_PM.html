PM wrap (prev close, Fri Jun 12): a broad, low-volatility melt-up. Net: disinflation has stalled in the 2.8-3.3% zone, leaving Warsh's hawkish FOMC every excuse to keep options open for a hike rather than cut — the single most important macro fact for the week ahead, with May CPI due alongside the meeting.

⚔️Iran War

After an early-June breakdown — a faltering ceasefire and renewed Israel-Iran strikes (Jun 7-8), followed by US strikes 'against multiple targets in Iran' (Jun 9-10) — momentum swung sharply toward de-escalation. On Jun 11, President Trump said a 'great settlement' had been reached to end the war (which began Feb 28) and expected signing within days. Per Iran's Mehr news agency (Jun 12), a draft US-Iran MOU includes Iran's commitment not to develop nuclear weapons, a permanent halt to the war on all fronts including Lebanon, a reopening of the Strait of Hormuz within 30 days, and a 60-day window to negotiate a final nuclear agreement. The US naval blockade of Iranian ports (in place since April) is to remain 'in full force' until a final deal. Markets read the de-escalation directly: WTI -3.23% and Brent -3.37%, with the war premium bleeding out — though the still-active blockade and the early-June ceasefire failure keep re-escalation a live tail risk.

He inherits spiking inflation and rising Treasury yields and a committee in no mood to ease — a setup where the hawkish risk is materially underpriced by a market sporting VIX 17.7 and record-tight credit.
