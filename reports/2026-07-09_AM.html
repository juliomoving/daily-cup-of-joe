The market impact is a rising energy risk premium (Brent +0.83% to 78.67, WTI +0.58% to 73.95) and a safe-haven bid in gold (+0.84%) and silver (+1.84%); equities and credit remain unusually calm, leaving a fat unhedged tail if Hormuz traffic is materially disrupted.

🏦Fed Chair Warsh
NEAR TERM

On hold at the next meeting, with the risk skewed to a hike rather than a cut. At his first meeting (June 17) Chair Warsh left rates unchanged, dropped forward guidance, and the SEP showed nearly the entire committee expecting a hike or hold — only one member penciled in a 2026 cut. Inference: no near-term easing; this is a signal, not a confirmed decision.

~1 YEAR

A gradual, data-contingent path toward modestly lower rates 'over time,' paired with a smaller balance sheet and a rethink of how the Fed measures inflation — but only once inflation credibly turns. With the 2026 outlook raised to ~3.6% headline / ~3.3% core, the bar to cut is high; a one-year horizon of roughly flat-to-slightly-lower policy is the base case. Flagged as inference from his on-record priorities, not a stated plan.

Warsh has framed price stability as the priority, calling post-pandemic inflation 'too high' and pledging the Fed 'will deliver price stability' with a 'strong, unanimous, and unambiguous' commitment.
