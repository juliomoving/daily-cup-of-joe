Persistence score: 63/100 — Sticky, not accelerating — and considerably stickier than the headline suggests. Breadth is the real problem and it is worse than the central tendency implies: the Dallas Fed distribution has 14.0% of the PCE basket rising faster than 10%, 10.0% in the 5-10% bucket and 34.0% in 3-5% — 58% of the basket running above 3% — against only 9.6% below 2% and 27.2% outright falling. Broad, not narrow. Bottom line: energy will flatter the next two CPI prints, but the sticky core sits near 2.8% with majority-of-basket breadth, and that is not a backdrop that gives Warsh room to cut.
