WHY TWO START DATES?

But 1990 predates the internet mania by ~4 years, so the second chart re-anchors the dot-com line to the 1994 reset low, just before the surge ignited, for a tighter like-for-like. IF THIS RHYMES WITH 2000
Nasdaq fell -78.0% peak→trough (2000-03-10 → 2002-10-09, ~31 months); it took ~15 years to reclaim the high.

The most 2000-like features today are not equity P/Es but the surrounding regime: record-tight credit, loose financial conditions, low vol, and capital crowded into US assets. CAPE near ~38x and a market-cap/GDP ('Buffett') ratio around ~205% (both analyst estimates) sit in the richest decile historically, and long-term EPS-growth optimism is elevated (~70/100, analyst estimate) — a classic BofA bear-market signpost. But the IPO market is only moderately frothy (~36/100, analyst estimate, with the live pipeline dominated by Hong Kong listings, not a US tech mania), and breadth/sentiment gauges are far from euphoric. The defensive sector rotation now underway is the kind of internal hedging that historically appears in the 6–12 months before a top, not at the euphoric peak itself. Watch semis P/E and credit spreads as the canaries.
